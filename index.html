👤 About Me

Who Am I?

I'm Diya Patel, an I.M.Sc. student in Cyber Security & Forensic Science at Gujarat University. I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying where they can fail, and learning how to make them more resilient.

My learning is grounded in practical work: building home labs, exploring web and network vulnerabilities, testing security controls, configuring firewall rules, and studying Active Directory environments. I have also taken part in hackathon projects, where I apply what I learn to practical problem-solving.

A key part of my hands-on learning has been building a virtual network independently: connecting Windows Server, Ubuntu, and Kali Linux virtual machines through an OPNsense firewall. I configured the lab environment, created an administrative user, worked with firewall rules and logs, and practised diagnosing connectivity issues between systems. This helped me understand how network segmentation, traffic flow, access control, and troubleshooting fit together.

I am currently deepening my knowledge of Security Operations Centre (SOC) workflows and networking through ongoing study and home-lab practice. I have foundational knowledge across a broad range of cybersecurity domains, including networking, Linux administration, web security, vulnerability assessment, SIEM/SOC concepts, cloud fundamentals, and digital forensics. I am developing these skills through hands-on projects and guided practice. I also use AI tools to support parts of my development workflow while reviewing and understanding the code I build.

I am seeking an internship where I can contribute, learn from experienced security professionals, and gain practical industry experience in a collaborative environment.
